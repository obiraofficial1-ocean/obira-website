Privacy Policy

Your trust matters to us. This policy explains what information OBIRA collects and how it is used.

Information We Collect

When you place an order or contact us, we may collect your name, email address, phone number, shipping address, order details and payment reference information.

How We Use Your Information

  • Process and deliver your orders.
  • Provide customer support and order updates.
  • Verify payments and help prevent misuse.
  • Improve our website, products and shopping experience.

Payments

Payments are handled by our payment gateway, Razorpay. OBIRA does not store your full card, UPI or banking credentials.

Sharing Information

We share only the information necessary with trusted payment, delivery and service providers to complete your order or provide support. We do not sell your personal information.

Our website may use browser storage, such as localStorage, to remember your cart and favourites. Cookies or similar technologies may be used to support site functionality and understand general website usage.

Data Protection

We use reasonable safeguards to protect your information. However, no online transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Your Choices

You may contact us to ask about the personal information associated with your order or to request help with an update or correction. Browser storage can be cleared through your browser settings, though this may remove saved cart and favourite items.
